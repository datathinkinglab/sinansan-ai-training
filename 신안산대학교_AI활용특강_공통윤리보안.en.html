Check your materials before giving them to AI

01Inputs &
attachments

Leave out real names, contact details, face photos and private files, and use public or made-up data.

02Storage &
training

For school or placement-site materials, follow their rules, and check your account’s history and training settings.

03Agent
permissions

Connect only the permissions you need. Before sharing or sending, personally check recipients, attachments and visibility.

Check the evidence before using AI output

01Facts &
calculations

Check numbers against raw data and formulas, quotes against the original, and dates against official notices.

02Copyright
& bias

Check the source and terms of use of text and images, and watch whether a certain appearance or family type is treated as the norm.

03Submitting
assignments

Check your professor’s rules on AI use and, if required, state specifically which parts AI helped with.
